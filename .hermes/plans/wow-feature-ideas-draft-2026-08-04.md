## What "wow wow" probably means for this user
- Not a productivity feature.
- Not a feature Google/Apple already demoed.
- Something emotionally profound, visually/conceptually stunning, or sci-fi-but-positive.
- Must leverage Aura's unique moat: deep personal memory + sideload freedom + no app-store policy constraints.

## Candidate directions to explore next
1. **Memory Time Machine** — talk to past versions of yourself (persona snapshots by life era).
2. **Reality Narrator** — Aura narrates your life in real-time like a movie trailer in your earbuds.
3. **Relationship Garden** — living visualization of your relationships, withering or thriving based on real interaction history.
4. **Predictive Life Script** — thousands of micro-simulations of your future, surfaced as surprising but plausible paths.
5. **Empathy Mirror** — simulate how your message will land emotionally on the recipient before you send it.
6. **Digital Afterlife / Legacy Mode** — package Aura into an artifact your family can run later.
